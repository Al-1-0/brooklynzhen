BIOGRAPHY

Brooklynzhen, the musical alias of Glasgow-based artist Allan McCafferty, who is a versatile musician, producer, and software developer. With a background in old-school-death-metal, Brooklynzhen has transitioned into the realms of underground house and techno, drawing inspiration from influential figures such as Sub club DJs, Daft Punk, The Field, and the French underground house scene.

In a world where diversity and division coexist, Brooklynzhen's music aims to create moments and memories that uplift spirits, making underground electronic music the sanctuary of happiness.

Brooklynzhen's debut single, "33rd Degree Jason," offered a unique blend of underground house and techno with a traditional song structure, making it easily accessible to a wide range of audiences.
