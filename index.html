BIOGRAPHY

Brooklynzhen, the musical alias of Glasgow-based artist Allan McCafferty is a versatile musician, producer, and software developer. With a background in old-school-death-metal, alternative rock, and electronica, Brooklynzhen has transitioned into the realms of live organic house and techno, drawing inspiration from influential figures such as The Field, Daft Punk, DJ Hell, and the French underground house scene.
