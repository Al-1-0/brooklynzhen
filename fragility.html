02 / ARTIST STATEMENT

The memory
of Earth.

Fragility is an original electronic composition and experimental environmental film exploring the vulnerable relationship between humanity, wildlife and the natural world.

Through thriving ecosystems, industrial expansion, deforestation and environmental destruction, the film follows the changing face of our planet.

Its closing movement, The Memory of Earth, reflects on landscapes transformed over decades — a quiet meditation on what remains, and what may disappear.

Music, editing and visual direction — Brooklynzhen
